# Code of Conduct

Be an adult.

- Do not harass people.
- Do not dump spam, crypto, or "AI agency" pitches in issues.
- Do not upload job-site photos or customer data "as an example."
- Political arguments belong on X, not in pull requests.
- Disagree on architecture. Do not make it personal.

Maintainers will close anything that wastes the time of people who
showed up to build. Repeat offenders get blocked.

Report abuse by opening a private security-style note in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or emailing the maintainer listed on the
GitHub profile.
