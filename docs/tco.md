# Three-year TCO

`eden tco` is a napkin, not a CFO model.

## Presets

- `phone-only` — you already own the work phone. Extra kWh from
  inference is the only new cost.
- `used-mini-pc` — a quiet used box, always-on-ish.
- `used-gpu-box` — used GPU, leave it running, pay the wall.

Cloud alternative defaults to $20/month. That is "one assistant
subscription," not "enterprise seats." Raise it if your real bill is
higher.

## What the napkin cannot see

- Time you waste fighting Termux after an OS update
- Time you save by not waiting on a usage reset
- The cost of a leaked job photo
- Opportunity cost of a model that is too dumb to draft a splice note

Privacy and outage resilience do not show up in `delta_usd`. They are
why the project exists anyway.
